How We Work

Transparent 4-Step Indenting Workflow

A seamless, transparent indenting procedure from specification submission to vessel discharge.

01

Mill Sourcing & Matching

Share your technical specifications (GSM, melt index, denier). We evaluate verified partner mills to secure direct Proforma Quotes.

02

Sample Testing & PI

We arrange mill samples for laboratory testing. Upon approval, a formal Proforma Invoice (PI) is issued directly in your company name.

03

LC Opening & Production

We assist in drafting compliant Letter of Credit (LC) clauses. The mill verifies the LC and initiates certified batch production.

04

Ocean Transit & Clearance

We monitor vessel progress to Chattogram/Mongla port and expedite shipping documents (BL, Packing List, CO) through banking channels.
